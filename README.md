# TrafficVision AI

> **Developed by Team 3**

TrafficVision AI is an AI-powered Smart Traffic Prediction and Congestion Management System designed to help city authorities monitor traffic conditions, predict congestion, and optimize traffic flow through intelligent data analysis. The platform combines real-time and historical traffic data to provide actionable insights, enabling proactive traffic management and supporting modern smart city initiatives.

The system offers a centralized dashboard for monitoring live traffic, forecasting congestion, analyzing traffic trends, recommending alternate routes, and generating alerts for accidents, road closures, and emergencies. By leveraging predictive analytics and interactive visualizations, TrafficVision AI helps improve urban mobility, reduce travel delays, and enhance road safety.

## Objectives

The primary goal of TrafficVision AI is to provide an intelligent platform for traffic monitoring and congestion management by:

- Predicting traffic congestion before it occurs.
- Improving urban traffic flow through AI-driven recommendations.
- Assisting authorities in making data-driven decisions.
- Reducing travel delays and congestion.
- Enhancing road safety through timely alerts.
- Supporting smart city transportation initiatives.

---

## System Workflow

1. Collect traffic data from multiple sources.
2. Monitor live traffic conditions.
3. Analyze traffic patterns using AI models.
4. Predict congestion and peak traffic periods.
5. Recommend optimized travel routes.
6. Generate alerts for incidents and traffic disruptions.
7. Present insights through interactive dashboards and reports.
